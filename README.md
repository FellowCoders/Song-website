
# 🎵 Music Site 

Welcome to my **Music Site** — a space where sound meets soul.

---

## 🌐 What is This?

This site is a digital home for music — mine, yours, ours, and everything in between.  
Whether you're here to **listen**, **discover**, **connect**, or just **vibe**, you're in the right place.

This project was built out of a love for melodies, rhythms, late-night playlists, and everything music makes us feel.

---

## 🔧 Features

- 🎧 **Music Player** – Stream tracks directly from the site.
- 📝 **Song Info** – Learn about each track: lyrics, inspiration, and credits.
- 📁 **Library** – Browse albums, singles, and featured playlists.
